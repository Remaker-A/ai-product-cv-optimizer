# Content Playbook

Use this file when rewriting resume content for AI product or AI product engineer roles.

## 10-Second Scan Structure

Top third of the resume should answer:

- Who is this candidate?
- What AI product direction do they fit?
- What are their strongest skills?
- What is their most credible shipped or high-signal result?
- Are they available for the role constraints?

Recommended order for one-page Chinese resumes:

1. Header: name, target title, contact, location, availability if important.
2. Summary: 2-3 lines, target-positioned.
3. Skills: grouped by capability, not a random keyword list.
4. Work experience: strongest real work first.
5. Project experience: modular cards or compact bullets by relevance.
6. Education: school, major, dates, GPA/rank/award if strong.

## Prioritization Rules

- Expand experiences with launch, adoption, business metrics, evaluation loops, or target-company relevance.
- Keep hackathons if they prove speed, product taste, AI capability, hardware integration, or award signal.
- Compress projects that are old, not target-relevant, unlaunched, or hard to explain in 10 seconds.
- Remove items that compete with the target direction or make the candidate look unfocused.
- Do not use equal length for all projects. Resume space should reflect signal strength.

## Bullet Formula

Use:

```text
动词 + 对象/场景 + 方法/机制 + 结果/指标
```

Better:

```text
主导客服 Agent 的知识库结构、Prompt、Memory 与输出前 LLM Check 优化，将人工介入率由 70%-80% 降至约 10%，准确率提升至 90%+。
```

Weak:

```text
负责客服 Agent 优化，提升回复效果。
```

## Section Templates

### Summary

```text
AI 产品工程师 / AI 产品经理，聚焦 {target_direction}。具备 {product_strength}、{technical_strength} 与 {delivery_strength}，曾在 {scenario} 中完成 {result_or_scale}。可 {availability_or_constraint}。
```

### Work Experience

```text
公司 | 职位 / 方向 | 时间 | 城市
- 产品职责：围绕 {scenario/problem}，负责 {scope}，完成 {prd/flow/prototype/agent workflow/model integration/evaluation/launch}。
- 业绩亮点：通过 {method} 达成 {metric/result}，并沉淀 {mechanism/system/process}。
```

### Project Card

```text
项目名 | 角色 | 时间 | 结果
职责：{personal_scope}，完成 {product/design/engineering/coordination}。
亮点：{metric/result/award/adoption}，体现 {target-relevant capability}。
```

## AI Product Expression Patterns

- Agentic workflow: "将 {process} 拆解为 {planner/tool/memory/eval/human review} 的 Agentic Workflow，并通过 {metric} 验证效果。"
- Model integration: "评估并接入 {model/tool}，设计其与现有 {canvas/workflow/platform} 的结合方式，完成 {user-facing capability}。"
- Evaluation: "建立 {case set/bad-case library/judge/rule-based checks}，覆盖 {coverage}，用于回归验证与持续迭代。"
- Demo-to-product: "在 {time} 内完成从产品定义、交互流程到前后端 Demo 的闭环，并在 {audience/judges/users} 中完成演示。"
- Multimodal product: "围绕 {input modality}->{output modality} 的创作链路，设计 {controls}，解决 {consistency/controllability/efficiency} 问题。"
- Enterprise automation: "将 {manual workflow} 自动化为 {system/agent}，处理 {volume}，将 {time/cost/error/intervention} 从 {before} 优化至 {after}。"

## Language Rules

- Prefer "主导 / 独立负责 / 负责 / 参与" only when ownership is accurate.
- Use "约 / 近 / 超过" for approximate metrics.
- Avoid "显著提升" unless followed by concrete data or a credible proxy.
- Avoid over-technical stacks unless the target is product engineering or developer tools.
- Keep bullets short enough to wrap cleanly; split long ideas into "职责" and "亮点" where layout benefits.
